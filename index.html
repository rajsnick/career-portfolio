Architecture & Business Logic

Multi-Contract Labor Classification & Cost Allocation

Evidence: E-0004, E-0005, E-0006, E-0007

The Challenge

Workforce operating across diverse labor agreements and branch structures experienced recurring SAP Infotype 0027 payroll gateway rejections due to unmapped AUF01 order numbers and effective-date sequencing mismatches.

The Action

Formulated Workday calculated-field precedence (LRVs, Evaluate Expressions) resolving labor classes dynamically. Separated volatile cost allocations into supplemental feeds and instituted mandatory gateway simulation checks.

The Outcome

Validated 100% end-to-end integration acceptance in ADP QA, authored standard operational runbooks for operations teams, and eliminated recurring payroll rejections in production cycles.

Data Reconciliation & Access

Cross-System Identity Linkage & Access Remediation

Evidence: E-0001, E-0002

The Challenge

Employees experienced ADP GlobalView login failures, profile dissociations, missing historical payslips, and authentication locks stemming from cross-system worker identifier mismatches with Workday HCM.

The Action

Led cross-functional triage with IT Security and ADP specialists, audited worker identifier mapping logic from Workday, and established an active exception register to track record remediation.

The Outcome

Restored self-service payslip access for impacted workforces and established pre-flight validation rules preventing future duplicate profile generation.
